FAQ

What's the difference between a vial and a kit?

A single vial is one sealed vial of the size you pick. A kit is ten vials of the same size and is priced much lower per vial.

How fast do orders ship?

Orders ship from US inventory, typically within one business day of payment confirmation. You'll receive tracking by email.

Do you offer bulk pricing?

Yes. Choose "Bulk quote" in the form with the compounds and quantities you need.

Are these for human use?

No. All products are sold strictly for laboratory research use only and are not for human or animal consumption.
